# RootKube Company Website

## Goal
Build the complete one-page RootKube website as a premium, dark, modern digital product engineering experience, using the supplied copy and avoiding invented company claims or contact details.

## Scope
- Replace the blank home page with a responsive, production-quality company website.
- Create a compact sticky navigation with polished mobile menu and section links.
- Build the cinematic opening section, animated capability marquee, editorial introduction, six services, business-problem mapping, featured work, delivery process, technology ecosystem, credibility, company story, and closing contact section/footer.
- Use abstract, custom-built technical visuals for the opening and case studies rather than stock photography.
- Add restrained scroll reveals, moving data paths, parallax-like case-study movement, hover interactions, and reduced-motion support.
- Use verified brief content only; represent unavailable metrics as capability statements and omit unverified contact details.
- Add page-specific search and social metadata.

## Visual Direction
- Near-black technical canvas with warm off-white text, cool cyan and electric lime accents, fine grid lines, thin borders, and selective translucent surfaces.
- Strong editorial typography, generous whitespace, asymmetric composition, and sharp low-radius geometry.
- Distinctive project visuals based on dashboards, document intelligence, and operational systems.

## Technical Details
- Keep the site at `/` with modular React sections and semantic HTML.
- Add Motion for React for accessible interactions and viewport-triggered sequences.
- Define all palette, surface, glow, and typography roles as semantic tokens in the global stylesheet.
- Preserve TanStack routing and update the root font links plus leaf-route metadata.
- Validate the final page at desktop and mobile sizes, including navigation and in-page actions.
